Entry scene notebook

A different way into each scene.

Choose your unit and format before writing. The notebook shows the notes relevant to your selection.

Acting

Keep the source details and your source check together. Describe a character’s feelings and the scene evidence that supports your choice.

Devising Drama

Record a chosen personal-experience theme and why it matters to your scene. Check current permitted themes with your teacher.

Miming

Give your mime a title and a reason. Note the imagined objects and movements you want to keep consistent.

Work alone.
Or listen for your partner.

Choose Solo or Duologue. Duologue adds a place for partner cues and shared actions; it does not create or send a shared online notebook.

A personal starting point

Start with a clear focus.

The scene title and practice focus are required. Build around them with location, the sequence of events and manually entered scene minutes. Entered minutes are your notes, not an automatic timer or an assessment score.

Notice one thing.
Choose one change.

Write what you or an observer noticed, then one change to try next time. Save keeps the current worksheet on your device. Reopening restores the last saved notes; it does not maintain multiple named notebooks or a version history.

Before your next rehearsal

Is this an official LAMDA app?

No. RushLabs develops this independent study app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by LAMDA or any government body. The app does not administer an examination, assess a performance or award a qualification.

Are the examples approved examination repertoire?

No. Original app examples are not automatically eligible repertoire. Confirm published-source conditions, permitted themes or titles and current examination requirements with your teacher and the official provider.

What happens to my scene notes?

The scene notebook stores a current worksheet locally when you choose Save. Reopening restores saved notes. There is no account, cloud synchronisation, partner messaging or version history. Keep your own copies of important work.

Can I download the app now?

Not yet. A paid download is planned with the included study content and scene notebook. No separate in-app purchase or subscription is enabled. Final price and regional availability will appear in the store when the app is released.
